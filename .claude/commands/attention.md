---
description: Everything that wants a decision today, worst first: aircraft that should not fly and why, then what is coming up, licences, quiet tech logs and bulletins to review.
---

1. Run `npm run mx -- attention`.
2. Read it in order:
   - **Airworthiness** first. Each line is an aircraft that should not fly, or flew when it should not have. Say which aircraft to hold on the ground today.
   - **Coming up.** Items inside the planning window and reviews of airworthiness to book.
   - **People.** Licences about to lapse.
   - **Records.** Active aircraft with no tech log entry for a week: the hours may be missing, so the due list is optimistic.
3. For each item give the one action and who owns it (engineer, chief pilot, office).
4. Anything that needs a letter or email is drafted to `drafts/`, never sent.

If the operator asks "what should I do today", pick the top three and say why those three.
