---
description: The due list: inspections, recurring directives and overhauls due within a window of hours or days, overdue first, with the date each falls due at the current rate of flying.
---

1. Run `npm run mx -- due` (default 50 hours or 60 days). Narrow it: `--reg=<reg>`, `--hours=25`, `--days=30`. Everything: `--all`.
2. Overdue items first, then by date. For hours-based items, say the projected date and that it assumes the last 30 days of flying continue.
3. Offer `/work-pack <reg>` for any aircraft with several items close together.
