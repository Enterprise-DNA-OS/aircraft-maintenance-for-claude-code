description: Draft the email that sends a work pack to an outside maintenance provider: the aircraft, hours, every item and directive to do, defects, and parts to order.
---

1. Run `npm run mx -- work-pack <reg> --json` and `npm run mx -- aircraft <reg> --json`.
2. Write `drafts/work-pack-<reg>-<date>.md`: who it is to (ask if you do not know the provider), the aircraft and its hours and cycles today, the items in a table with their references, directives to comply with, defects with their numbers, parts to have on hand, and when the aircraft can be there.
3. Plain and short. Never send it. Say where the draft is.
